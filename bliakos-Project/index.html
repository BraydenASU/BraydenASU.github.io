Welcome to "What Should I Play?"!

Here’s a brief rundown of the categories:

  • Casual – Easier & relaxing games; you're playing to chill out!
  • Competitive – Multiplayer games; test your skills against others!
  • Story-based – Narrative-focused; immerse yourself in rich storytelling!
  • Hardcore – Challenging games; test your limits!
  • Play with friends – Co-op & multiplayer; enjoy gaming together!
